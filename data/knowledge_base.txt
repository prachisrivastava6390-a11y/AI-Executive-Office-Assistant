PRACHIVA OFFICE SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD.
FICTIONAL COMPANY - TEST DATA

COMPANY OVERVIEW

Prachiva Office Solutions Pvt. Ltd. is a fictional technology and business-services company created for the AI Executive Office Assistant project.

The company has departments including Human Resources, Finance, Information Technology, Operations, Engineering, AI and Data, and Sales.

The AI Executive Office Assistant helps employees retrieve internal information, understand company policies, summarize information, and perform selected office tasks.

WORKING HOURS AND ATTENDANCE

Standard working hours are Monday to Friday, 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM.

Employees are expected to be available during their assigned working hours.

Employees should record their attendance through the company's attendance system.

Employees who expect to arrive late should inform their reporting manager as early as possible.

Repeated attendance issues may be reviewed by the employee's manager and Human Resources department.

Employees working in different shifts must follow the working hours assigned to their team.

LEAVE POLICY

Employees can request annual leave, sick leave, and personal leave through the internal leave system.

Leave requests should normally be submitted at least two working days before the planned leave.

Emergency or sick leave should be communicated to the reporting manager as soon as possible.

Leave approval depends on business requirements, team availability, and manager approval.

Employees should check their available leave balance before submitting a planned leave request.

WORK FROM HOME POLICY

Employees may work from home when their role and team requirements allow it.

Work-from-home requests should be submitted through the approved internal process.

Employees working from home must remain available during their scheduled working hours.

Employees must have a suitable internet connection and a secure working environment.

Confidential company information must not be accessed from unsecured public computers.

MEETING POLICY

Employees should join scheduled meetings on time.

Meeting invitations should include the meeting purpose, date, time, and required participants.

Participants should review relevant documents before important meetings.

The meeting organizer should share important action items after the meeting when required.

Meetings should be cancelled when they are no longer necessary.

EMAIL AND COMMUNICATION POLICY

Employees should use their official company email account for business communication.

Important business communication should be written clearly and professionally.

Confidential information should only be shared with authorized recipients.

Employees should avoid sending sensitive company information through personal email accounts.

Employees should respond to important internal requests within a reasonable business timeframe.

IT SUPPORT POLICY

Employees should contact the IT Support team when they experience problems with company devices, applications, network access, or business software.

For urgent technical problems that prevent an employee from working, the employee should mark the issue as high priority through the IT support system.

Employees should not install unauthorized software on company devices.

Employees should not share their company passwords with other employees.

Company laptops and other IT equipment should be handled carefully and returned when requested.

EXPENSE AND REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

Employees may request reimbursement for approved business expenses.

Expense claims should include the date, amount, business purpose, and supporting receipt when applicable.

Employees should submit expense claims within 15 calendar days of the expense.

Business expenses must be reasonable and related to approved company activities.

The employee's manager must approve applicable expenses before reimbursement is processed.

Reimbursement requests without sufficient supporting information may be returned for clarification.

BUSINESS TRAVEL POLICY

Business travel should be approved by the employee's reporting manager before booking.

Employees should use approved travel options and follow company travel guidelines.

Travel expenses should be supported by valid receipts whenever required.

Employees should submit travel expense claims within 15 calendar days after returning from the business trip.

Any major change to an approved business trip should be communicated to the reporting manager.

EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING

New employees complete an onboarding process coordinated by Human Resources.

The onboarding process may include identity verification, employment documentation, company policy acknowledgement, system access setup, and introduction to the employee's team.

IT Support is responsible for providing required company systems and equipment.

New employees should complete mandatory training assigned during onboarding.

Employees should contact Human Resources if they have questions about onboarding documentation.

EMPLOYEE OFFBOARDING

Employees leaving the company must complete the required exit process.

Company equipment such as laptops, access cards, and other assigned assets must be returned according to company instructions.

Access to company systems may be disabled as part of the offboarding process.

Employees should complete required knowledge-transfer activities before their final working day.

Human Resources coordinates the exit documentation and related formalities.

INFORMATION SECURITY

Employees are responsible for protecting company information.

Company passwords must not be shared with other people.

Employees should use strong and unique passwords for company accounts.

Suspicious emails, links, or attachments should be reported to the IT Support or Information Security team.

Confidential information should only be accessed by authorized employees.

Employees should lock their computers when leaving their workspace.

Employees must not copy confidential company information to unauthorized personal storage devices.

DOCUMENT MANAGEMENT

Company documents should be stored in approved company storage systems.

Documents should use clear and meaningful file names.

Employees should avoid creating unnecessary duplicate copies of important documents.

Confidential documents should only be shared with authorized employees.

Important documents should be reviewed for accuracy before being distributed.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Question: What are the standard working hours?

Answer: Standard working hours are Monday to Friday, 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM.

Question: How should I request leave?

Answer: Employees should submit a leave request through the internal leave system. Planned leave should normally be submitted at least two working days in advance.

Question: What should I do if I have a laptop problem?

Answer: Contact the IT Support team and create an IT support request. If the problem prevents you from working, mark the issue as high priority.

Question: Can I work from home?

Answer: Work from home may be available when the employee's role and team requirements allow it. Employees should follow the approved work-from-home process.

Question: When should I submit an expense claim?

Answer: Employees should normally submit expense claims within 15 calendar days of the expense.

Question: Can I share my company password with my colleague?

Answer: No. Company passwords must not be shared with other employees.

Question: Who handles employee onboarding?

Answer: Human Resources coordinates the onboarding process, while IT Support handles required systems and equipment.

Question: What happens during offboarding?

Answer: Employees complete the required exit process, return company assets, complete knowledge transfer, and finish required documentation.
